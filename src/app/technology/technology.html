Strata

Financial diligence built for buy-and-build.

PORTFOLIO

Measure each target against what you own.

Every engagement sits on one platform, with headline metrics across the portfolio and sector benchmarks drawn from completed and in-flight QoEs.

DATA UPLOAD

One intake for the full data request.

Files upload in any format or sync from QuickBooks Online, NetSuite and Sage, tracked against a document checklist.

FINANCIAL FOUNDATION

One chart of accounts for every target.

Every account is mapped to a standard structure by period, entity and location, viewable annually or monthly, in dollars or as a percentage of revenue.

QUALITY OF EARNINGS

Every adjustment, fully supported.

Management, due diligence and pro forma adjustments, each with its methodology, the deal team’s commentary and its impact on EBITDA.

TRANSACTION ISSUES

The findings that move price or terms.

Each risk is written up with what was found, why it matters and the recommended path forward.

VALUATION

The price, tested under every EBITDA case.

The purchase price measured against underwritten, management-adjusted, QoE and synergy-adjusted EBITDA.

REPORTING

A databook and an IC-ready deck.

The Excel databook and the FDD presentation generate from the same data, so every figure ties back to the source.

Sourcing OS

Target sourcing and owner outreach for private equity.

INTAKE

Start from an approved brief.

Every search begins with an intake form: sector, consolidation landscape, target criteria and geography, approved before any data is collected.

SCRAPE

Map the entire market.

Keywords, states and exclusion terms run across every source, and results are deduplicated into one clean table.

TRANSFORMATION

Clean it into one table.

Raw results are cleaned into a single table, with website, reviews, rating, category and social profiles for every company.

INTELLIGENCE

See every target on the map.

Targets are placed by metro area, so the team can see where companies cluster and which have been contacted.

TIERING

Rank the market with AI.

Describe the ideal target in plain language. AI turns it into weighted criteria and ranks every company into Tier 1, 2 and 3, with each weight adjustable before it is applied.

ENRICH

Reach the decision-makers.

Owners and operators are matched to work email and phone, row by row, from the sources you choose.

OUTREACH

Sequence through the tiered list.

Email sequences run through the tiered list, starting with Tier 1, and every send, reply and opportunity is tracked by campaign.

    Platform overview

    Each deal makes the next one faster.

    Every diligence engagement answers the same questions: how the accounts map, which earnings adjustments apply, what drives performance and what the documents support. Strata keeps that work in one database, so the next target starts from what was already built.

    • Source exports parse straight into a standardized GL and trial balance
    • Dashboards and insights ready as soon as data enters the build
    • One live Databook, no version management or workbook links required
    • Active deals benchmarked against every deal delivered
    Architecture

    What’s inside Strata.

    For repeat acquirers

    Benchmarks improve with every acquisition.

    Launch each engagement from an established baseline.

    Recurring accounting treatments, cost categories and operating patterns become easier to recognize deal after deal. STOC starts from proven data structures and prior analysis, then investigates what is specific to the new target.

    Measure each target against the existing portfolio.

    A higher margin becomes a question to answer. Does it reflect a different service mix, a more efficient model, or a difference in how costs are recorded? A common financial structure makes the comparison possible.

    Maintain the depth behind the benchmark.

    Historical financials, recast statements, adjustments and schedules sit in one place, so any gap against the portfolio can be traced to the accounts behind it.

    Shorten the timeline on every acquisition.

    Each engagement starts from existing templates and prior work, so it launches faster and takes less time to review.

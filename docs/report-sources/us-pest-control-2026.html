Executive Summary
84.8% of U.S. Pest Control Locations Are Still Independent
16,910 independent locations, 45 named buyer groups, and the runway between them in every state and metro.
The STOC View
STOC classified 19,935 pest control locations by ownership. 3,025 (15.2%) sit inside 45 platforms, chains, and franchise networks, and the five largest groups hold 48.5% of that footprint. The other 16,910 locations (84.8%) are independent. Even the largest state markets stay open: California is 92.4% independent, Texas 87.2%, Florida 84.9%. In a $29.7B industry where 74% of revenue is recurring, entry comes down to two numbers in each market: the independent share, and who owns the rest. Both numbers follow market by market.

Methodology Appendix
Methodology, Definitions, and Verification Framework
The methodology distinguishes independent supply, platform and network activity, CBSA market classifications, ownership verification, and supporting geographic screens. The definitions below establish the denominator, scope, and interpretation used for each analytical component.
Topic Methodology note
Exhibit denominatorsEach exhibit uses the denominator stated on the page. Ownership comparisons use the 19,935 records with an identified ownership type. Supply and ZIP analyses use included observed records. Distance analyses use records with usable coordinates.
Independent locations and operatorsKept separate from platform / chain / network context so independent supply depth is evaluated on its own. Individual independent operators are anonymized throughout.
Ownership verification frameworkNamed institutional groups (public chains, PE-backed platforms, family / private consolidators, franchise networks, and the founder-owned network) are classified from public sources and marked Verified (primary source: sponsor, company, press release, or SEC filing) or Reported (credible secondary source such as trade press). Each named group carries a source link and source date where available, plus a verification status; all rows were last cross-checked in August 2026. Ownership of individual independent operators is not verified; they are anonymized and excluded from named claims.
Observed vs. known universeThe 45 named groups reflect STOC’s observed-location universe, not a complete buyer census. Active acquirers with little or no observed footprint (for example Senske Services, backed by GTCR) are tracked on a separate watchlist and are not counted in the 45.
Observed operator groupsNational operator-group totals use the source-provided operator-size field. The operator-group identifier is not available for geographic reporting, so distinct operator-group counts are not reported by state or CBSA. Metro-level Buyer Groups counts refer to the 45 named parent / network groups, which are reported geographically.
Operator segmentsThe independent base is grouped into three mutually exclusive operator segments by operator scale, specialty signal, and public-profile coverage; each independent location appears in exactly one segment.
Established Add-On MarketsCBSAs with at least 100 independent locations, at least 10% network share, and at least 10 observed parent companies or networks. The five market groups are assigned sequentially: Established Add-On (100+ independents, 10%+ network share, 10+ parent groups); Large Independent / Low Network (100+ independents, share below 10%); High Network Presence (50+ independents, share 20%+); Mid-Sized Independent (remaining 50+); Smaller Observed (all others). 22 + 7 = 29 priority metros.
Provider-density and proximityStraight-line proximity between observed provider locations, used as a market-screening lens. It does not measure customer routes, drive times, technician territories, or integration economics.
Visible supply-gap signalHigher-population ZIPs containing only one or two observed provider locations. Validation candidates, not confirmed unmet-demand or zero-provider markets. ZIP population is a local density proxy supplied in the source dataset.
Visible service-line signalsTermite, mosquito, wildlife, rodent, and bed bug indicators reflect visible profile and category signals rather than confirmed service revenue mix.
Digital-presence tiersSource-provided relative classifications indicating public-profile coverage. They support research prioritization and do not represent owner or direct-contact availability, revenue, quality, or willingness to sell.
Runway metricRunway = independent share of classified locations in a geography (100% minus network share). Consolidated and network are used interchangeably for locations inside the 45 named platform, chain, and franchise groups. It measures ownership headroom only; it does not measure demand, pricing, or operator quality.
State and metro tablesState rows use all classified records with a state assignment and reconcile to 16,910 independent and 3,025 network locations. Metro rows use CBSA-coded records; totals are whole-metro counts, multi-state metros appear under each member state, and 152 classified records without a metro code appear in state totals only.
Interactive exhibitsThe web edition of this report provides state-level metro breakdowns from the National Supply Map and column sorting on the State Opportunity Table. The printed edition presents all exhibits at the state level.
Geography treatmentState and regional views use records with state assignments; CBSA views use CBSA-coded records; ZIP screens use ZIP-coded records. Four regions are used: Northeast, Midwest, South, and West. Distances are great-circle miles.
Source dates. Proprietary pest control location dataset: July 2026. Named-platform ownership: cross-checked against public sources as of August 2026 (see Named Ownership Appendix). Industry context: Gwyneth Feroleto, Pest Control in the US, IBISWorld, March 2026, NAICS 56171, used for industry size and recurring-revenue context only. External figures are never combined with proprietary location counts.
Basis and limits. All rankings, supply clusters, market classifications, operator segments, and supply-gap signals are derived from the proprietary pest control location dataset. Ownership classifications for all 45 named institutional groups are supported by cited public sources as of August 2026. Independent-operator ownership is not individually verified and is anonymized. The analysis is not a complete legal-entity census and does not establish revenue mix, customer mix, operating performance, or transaction readiness. Recipients should apply independent judgment and conduct their own diligence.
